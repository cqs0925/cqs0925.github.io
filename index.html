Final-year PhD student · University of Bristol

Hello! I am a final-year PhD student in the MaVi Group at the University of Bristol, advised by Prof. Majid Mirmehdi, Dr Catherine Morgan and Prof. Alan Whone.

My research focuses on computer vision and its applications in human motion analysis, including 3D human modeling, action recognition and detection, and multimodal indoor healthcare monitoring. I am grateful to be supported by the EPSRC Centre for Doctoral Training Studentship and the TORUS project.
